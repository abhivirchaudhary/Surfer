# Surfer privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Surfer is a web browser for iPhone and iPad, made by Abhivir. This page says what Surfer does with your information.

## The short version

Surfer has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. I do not run any server that Surfer talks to, and I do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

## What stays on your device

Surfer keeps these on your device only: your open tabs (if you choose "Continue where you left off"), browsing history, bookmarks and folders, reading list and the saved copies of pages, files you download, your settings, per-site settings, your userscripts, and your favourites. Private tabs are never saved: when you close them, what they stored is deleted. You can clear history, cookies and site data at any time in Settings → Privacy, or forget a single site in Settings → Sites.

## What leaves your device, and where it goes

- **The websites you visit.** Like any browser, Surfer connects to the sites you open. Those sites and your network provider see your visit, as they would in any browser. Surfer blocks many ads and trackers and can upgrade connections to https.
- **Filter lists.** Surfer downloads public ad-blocking lists from raw.githubusercontent.com and, if that fails, cdn.jsdelivr.net. Those services can see your device's internet address and that a list was requested. No personal information is sent.
- **Search suggestions.** If "Search suggestions" is on, the text you type in the address bar is sent to your chosen search engine so that it can suggest searches. Turn it off in Settings → Search to stop this.
- **Translate Page.** If you choose it, the page's address is sent to Google's translation service.
- **Userscripts you install.** A userscript can make its own network requests and can ask for extra files it needs. Install only scripts you trust.
- **iCloud, if you turn it on.** Surfer can keep your bookmarks, reading list entries, site settings and userscripts in sync across your devices using your iCloud account. That data is stored by Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Turn this off in Settings → iCloud.
- **Handoff.** The address of the page you are viewing (never a private tab) can be offered to your other Apple devices through Apple's Handoff.

## Camera, microphone and location

Websites can ask to use the camera, microphone or your location. Surfer asks you, and you can set Allow, Ask or Block for each site. The QR scanner uses the camera on your device and does not keep or send what it sees. Page summaries are written by the language model built into your iPhone; the page text does not leave your device.

## Children

Surfer is a general web browser and is not directed at children. It lets people open any website.
